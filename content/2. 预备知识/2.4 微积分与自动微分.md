1. 鑷姩寰垎

```python
x = torch.arange(4.0, requires_grad=True)
y = 2 * torch.dot(x, x)
print(y)
# tensor(28., grad_fn=<MulBackward0>)

y.backward()      # 璁＄畻姊害
print(x.grad)     # 璇诲彇姊害
# tensor([ 0.,  4.,  8., 12.])

```
